What to Have Ready When You Call

  • What’s happening with your door. Won’t open, won’t close, stuck partway, making an unusual noise, off its track: a quick description helps us arrive prepared.
  • Roughly how old your door and opener are, if you know.
  • Your address, or a clear description of your location. A precise address is ideal, but a description relative to a well-known local landmark, such as Wildwood Park, the Oaks Mall, or a cross street off Thousand Oaks Blvd or Moorpark Rd, works fine too.
  • Whether it’s urgent. If you can’t get your car out, or you’re concerned about security with a door that won’t close, let us know so we can prioritize accordingly.
  • If it’s related to recent Santa Ana wind activity. Wind-driven debris damage or dust intrusion is a common seasonal pattern we’re well set up to diagnose quickly.
