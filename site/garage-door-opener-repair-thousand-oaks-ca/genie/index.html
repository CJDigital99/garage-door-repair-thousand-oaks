Home / Garage Door Opener Repair / Genie

Genie openers are common across a number of Thousand Oaks’ established neighborhoods, and while they’re generally durable units, age-related wear on the drive system and circuit board are the most frequent reasons we get called out to service them.

Common Genie Issues We Fix

  • Drive system wear, leading to noise or inconsistent lifting performance.
  • Circuit board faults, causing intermittent or complete unresponsiveness, sometimes accelerated by years of heat cycling in an uninsulated garage.
  • Remote and wall control issues, sometimes a simple battery or programming fix, sometimes indicating a deeper unit fault.
  • Force and travel limit drift, causing a door that doesn’t fully open or close.

Our Diagnostic Approach

We assess whether your Genie opener’s symptoms genuinely originate with the unit or with another part of the system: springs, cables, and sensors can all present symptoms that look like an opener problem at first glance.

Repair or Replace?

We’ll give you an honest recommendation based on your specific Genie unit’s age and condition: repair is usually the better value for units under roughly 8 to 10 years old, while older or repeatedly-failing units often make more sense to replace outright.

What Genie Repair Typically Costs

Most Genie repairs are straightforward, same-day fixes completed in a single visit, since we carry common parts for this brand along with the others we service most frequently.

Frequently Asked Genie Repair Questions

How long do Genie openers typically last?

With reasonable maintenance, many years, but age-related wear eventually catches up, particularly on units original to older Thousand Oaks homes.

Can you repair an older Genie model, or only newer units?

We service the full range, and can advise honestly if parts for a particularly old model are becoming hard to source.
